🤔 What Is This?

laya-jev-GraphRAG is a smart layer that sits on top of your existing data. Instead of needing to program complex searches or AI connections, this tool does the heavy lifting for you. It's like having a personal AI assistant that can answer questions using the information stored in your graphs, without you needing to know any code.

Think of it as a translator between plain English questions and your database. You ask something naturally, and it finds the best answer from your data — fast and accurate.

✨ Key Features

🔀 Swappable Intelligence

Use the free, local Laya model when you want privacy and offline ability. Switch to the more powerful cloud Jev model when you need enhanced performance. You can change anytime!

🔄 Works with Any Graph Database

Neo4j, Amazon Neptune, JanusGraph — doesn't matter. Our framework speaks the universal language of graph databases, so you're never locked in.

🧭 Smart Search (A* Algorithm)

Our custom-built A* traversal finds the most efficient path to answers. It's like having a GPS for your data — always finding the quickest route to the information you need.

📊 Built-in Quality Check

Every answer goes through a continuous evaluation system, so you always get high-quality, trustworthy responses. No more guessing if the answer is right!

🔒 Privacy-First Option

With the local Laya model, your data never leaves your machine. Perfect for sensitive information or offline environments.

🛠️ Four-Step Pipeline

A complete, optimized 4-phase process that handles everything from understanding your question to delivering the answer. You don't need to configure anything — it just works.

🔍 How It Works

laya-jev-GraphRAG functions in four simple phases, entirely automated behind the scenes:

  1. Ask: You type a natural language question (e.g., "Which customers bought products last month?")
  2. Understand: The system interprets your intent using either the local or cloud AI model.
  3. Search: Using the A* algorithm, it efficiently navigates your graph database to find relevant connections.
  4. Answer: It presents a clear, concise response with the information you need.

💡 Pro tip: You don't need to write any queries or complex syntax. Just ask naturally, and laya-jev-GraphRAG handles the rest.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I connect my database?

The app provides a simple connection wizard. You enter your database address and credentials, and it takes care of the rest.

Do I need a powerful computer?

Not at all! If you use the cloud Jev model, all the heavy processing happens on our servers. Local Laya mode is optimized to work on any standard laptop.

Is my data safe?

Yes! With Laya mode, your data stays 100% on your machine. With Jev mode, data is sent only to answer your current question and is never stored.

Can I use it with my existing AI tools?

Absolutely. It's built to work alongside other systems. You can use it as a standalone tool or integrate it as part of a larger workflow.
